About Global Delivery Services
Global Delivery Services refers to EY's worldwide network of service delivery
centers. The GDS team plays an important role in EY's strategy by ensuring
effective support to EY's growth agenda. Our journey started in 2002 with
approximately 200 people. Today we stand at 80,000+ professionals in ten
locations around the world: Argentina, China, Hungary, India, Philippines,
Poland, Sri Lanka, Mexico, Spain and the United Kingdom.

Client service is focused on providing Consulting, Assurance, Tax, Strategy &
Transactions, and Knowledge support to our clients around the world.

The Opportunity
Our Consulting practice provides differentiated focus on the key business
themes to help our clients solve better questions around technology. We are
looking for highly motivated, articulate individuals who have the skills to
the technology lifecycle and are passionate about designing innovative
solutions to solve complex business problems.

Technology Areas / Service Lines
Your career in Consulting can span across:

- Testing Services: Business/User acceptance testing, functional, technical

- Data & Analytics: Information Management (IM), Business Intelligence (BI),
  Advance Analytics (AA) and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- Oracle: SQL or PL/SQL, Java, JavaScript, Python, IBM Maximo and other
  Oracle Utilities, Enterprise Performance Management tools.
- SAP: S/4HANA digital core and cloud services, Technology implementation

- Banking and Capital Market Services: AI and ML for tax and finance

- Wealth and Asset Management: Technology, data and digital teams for
  operational efficiency programs and Technology Enabled Transformation.
- Insurance Transformation: IoT, autonomous vehicles, Blockchain, services

  brokers.
- Cyber Security: Understanding and quantifying cyber risk, embedding
  security, privacy and resilience into digitally-enabled initiatives.
- Technology Risk: IT Risk and Assurance client engagements, internal and
  external relationships, identifying risks within engagements.
