National Stock Exchange Limited
Technology Job Description

Who we are
A homegrown brand with a global vision, NSE is counted as one of the world's
largest exchanges and a catalyst for driving India's economic growth. NSE was
the first exchange in India to implement electronic or screen-based trading
which began its operations in 1994; a pioneer in technology which ensures
the reliability and performance of its systems through a culture of
innovation and investment in technology.

Role: Technology Operations
Location: Chennai

You will be responsible for
Performing Application IT Operations as per defined operational processes
and tools.

Knowledge Management:
- Read technology documentation to understand scope of work (SOW).
- Awareness of application being handled, its key components and business
  flows.
- Support scope and actions to be performed during Production releases,
  mock drills, DR drills and LIVE sessions.

Monitoring and Event Management:
- Perform monitoring and checklists of Production environment comprising of
  Linux/Windows based applications.
- Identify and Review system Events/Alerts to be covered for monitoring.

Incident and Problem Management:
- Production Incident troubleshooting -- Escalate and resolve.
- Perform incident analysis as part of operational efficiency.
- Identify repetitive Incidents - Create and assign Problem Tickets to L3
  teams.

Service Request Management:
- Fulfil end user requests received via Ticketing Tool.
- Timely closure and/or escalation of service requests.

Change Management:
- Provide operations signoff to Development Team as part of Application
  releases/changes.
- Perform production changes basis guidelines & SOP documents received from
  Development team.
- Monitor change wrt Success and Failure -- Escalate and perform revert of
  Production changes as per SOP document.
- Create & review of release scripts/utilities for operational efficiency.

Stakeholder Communication:
- Follow established escalation matrix and procedures.
- Conduct periodic service review call with all internal & external teams
  comprising of technology, business and/or partner teams.

Security and Compliance:
- Strict adherence to Organization's Security and Compliance guidelines.
- Keep updated of the changes to policies and procedures.

Skill Set Required
- Knowledge of Linux based OS concepts, IPC, Linux commands, Java/C,
  Scripting, SQL/PLSQL, RDBMS and code release methodologies.
- Knowledge of client server and Java based 3 tier applications in
  Linux/Windows.
- Knowledge of complete software development cycle, Maintenance and
  Operations.
- Knowledge of latest Java technologies/framework, AWS, Cloud Computing etc.
- Expertise in application monitoring concepts and Tools.
- ITIL process understanding.
- Knowledge of various automation tool (ITRS, ServiceNow, Control M etc).

What we have to offer you
- A Dynamic and Inspiring work environment.
- Collaborative and inclusive work culture that values Diversity,
  Innovation, and Continuous learning.
- Ample opportunities to work on challenging projects for professional
  growth and advancement.
- Support for professional development.
- Many more Employee Benefits and Perks.
