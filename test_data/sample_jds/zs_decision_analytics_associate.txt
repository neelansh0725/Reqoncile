Decision Analytics Associate
ZS
Location: Pune | Gurugram

ZS is a place where passion changes lives. As a management consulting and
technology firm focused on improving life and how we live it, we transform
ideas into impact by bringing together data, science, technology and human
ingenuity to deliver better outcomes for all.

Here you'll work side-by-side with a powerful collective of thinkers and
experts shaping life-changing solutions for patients, caregivers and
consumers, worldwide. ZSers drive impact by bringing a client-first mentality
to each and every engagement.

The Insights & Analytics group is a dynamic collective of thinkers and
experts. We learn quickly and are committed to using data to drive better
outcomes for all. By partnering with clients to design solutions rooted in
advanced data analytics -- augmented by AI and creative problem-solving -- we
bring passion and purpose to making a life-changing impact every day.

Decision Analytics Associates gain hands-on experience from day one.
Learning from smart, experienced, and supportive mentors, you'll rapidly
build consulting and advanced analytics skills. Real-time feedback, a focus
on analytical upskilling and direct exposure to client challenges ensure
fast learning and continuous improvement.

What you'll do
- Tell powerful stories with data: Develop robust analytical models from
  complex data in healthcare and other industries, uncovering compelling
  and actionable insights.
- Drive client decisions: Present your findings to clients, empowering them
  to make intelligent, data-driven strategic decisions.
- Custom solutioning: Collaborate daily with consultants, data engineers and
  domain experts to design the best-fit solutions.
- Ongoing development: Continuously enhance your skills by learning new
  tools and sharpening your critical thinking in a fast-paced environment.

What you'll bring
- Results-oriented: Client-focused and outcome-driven self-starter,
  comfortable with complexity and ambiguity.
- Tech savvy: Demonstrated ability to quickly learn new technologies,
  analytical approaches and advancements in AI.
- Critical thinker: Strong critical thinking and problem-solving skills,
  ready to tackle complex consulting challenges.
- Clear communicator: Excellent presentation and interpersonal skills, with
  the ability to translate complex data into actionable insights.
- Collaborative & inclusive: A team player who values diverse perspectives
  and contributes positively.

How you'll grow: Cross-functional skills development & custom learning
pathways, milestone training programs aligned to career progression
opportunities, internal mobility paths.

Hybrid working model: ZSers are on-site at clients or ZS offices three days
a week, with flexibility to work remotely two days a week.
