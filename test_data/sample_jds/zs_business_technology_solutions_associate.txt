Business Technology Solutions Associate
ZS
Location: Pune | Gurugram

ZS is a place where passion changes lives. As a management consulting and
technology firm focused on improving life and how we live it, we transform
ideas into impact by bringing together data, science, technology and human
ingenuity to deliver better outcomes for all.

What you'll do
- Gain a deep understanding of our clients, their business, domain and data
  by working closely with ZS experts.
- Collaborate with ZS and client teams at the intersection of data, domain,
  technology and AI to design and implement impactful solutions.
- Continuously learn and apply new tools and technologies to enhance
  processes and drive client value.
- Develop, test and deploy technology solutions using varied tools and tech
  stacks, following ZS's standard delivery practices and methodologies
  (e.g., Agile, Waterfall).
- Contribute to key project deliverables such as business cases, solution
  designs, user requirements, prototypes, technical architecture, test
  cases and operational support.
- Assist in planning and managing project deliverables while supporting
  audit, compliance and regulatory requirements.
- Ensure transparency and timely communication with stakeholders,
  highlighting risks and issues proactively.
- Provide post-production support and collaborate effectively with team
  members.
- Demonstrate flexibility, strong task management and a consulting mindset
  by combining technical and business expertise to solve problems and
  drive innovation.

What you'll bring
- Strong verbal and written communication skills with ability to articulate
  results and issues to internal and client teams.
- Proven ability to work creatively and analytically in a problem-solving
  environment.
- Passion and self-drive for learning new tools, technologies, programming
  languages, domain and client's issue areas.
- Basic understanding of algorithms, data structures, programming skills,
  relational databases and data warehousing, cloud computing concepts are
  preferred.
- Ability to work within a virtual global team environment and contribute
  to the overall timely delivery of multiple projects.
- Willingness to travel to other global offices as needed to work with
  clients or other internal project teams.
- ZS is a global consulting firm; English fluency is required. Additional
  fluency in at least one European or Asian language is highly desired.

How you'll grow: Cross-functional skills development & custom learning
pathways, milestone training programs aligned to career progression
opportunities, internal mobility paths that empower growth via s-curves,
individual contribution and role expansions.

Hybrid working model: ZSers are on-site at clients or ZS offices three days
a week, with flexibility to work remotely two days a week.

To complete your application: Candidates must possess or be able to obtain
work authorization for their intended country of employment. Contact your
Training and Placement Office for next steps and eligibility criteria.
